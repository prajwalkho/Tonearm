Tonearm app player check

This page observes the actual Tonearm player in the embedded app. Start recording, then use the app below as a listener would. It checks selected-song/UI agreement, playback state, seek, volume, Next/Previous, Shuffle/Repeat state, and reports browser errors. Browser autoplay rules still apply, so begin playback with a direct click in the embedded app.

Waiting for app

Serve the project root over HTTP, then open /scripts/app-player-check.html. The embedded app must be same-origin with this page. Interact with real songs and controls. A row selection or control is counted only after the resulting app/audio state is observed. This tool does not claim to certify all browsers or replace the per-file audio check.
